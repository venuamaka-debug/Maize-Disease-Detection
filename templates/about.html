Maize Leaf Detection Field diagnostic tool
Detect About

Purpose

This tool helps identify common maize leaf conditions from a single photograph, using a convolutional neural network trained on labelled leaf images. It's intended as a decision-support aid, not a replacement for expert diagnosis.

Conditions detected

  • Northern Corn Leaf BlightCigar-shaped grey-green lesions
  • Common RustReddish-brown raised pustules
  • Gray Leaf SpotRectangular tan-grey lesions
  • HealthyNo signs of the above

How to get a good result

Photograph a single leaf in good light, filling most of the frame, with the affected area (if any) clearly visible against a plain background where possible.

Limitations

This tool is not always certain. When it isn't confident enough in a result, it will tell you instead of guessing. It also works best on maize leaves specifically, so a photo of a different plant may not be read correctly. This tool is meant to support your decisions, not replace advice from an agricultural expert, especially before using any fungicide or chemical treatment.
