About this system

This tool classifies photographs of maize leaves into one of four categories using a convolutional neural network trained on labelled leaf images: Northern Corn Leaf Blight, Common Rust, Gray Leaf Spot, and Healthy.

How to use it

Upload a clear, well-lit photo of a single leaf. The system works best when the leaf fills most of the frame and shows the affected area clearly.

Limitations

This is a classification aid, not a diagnostic authority. Results below the confidence threshold are flagged as inconclusive rather than shown as definitive. For agronomic decisions, consult a qualified expert.
